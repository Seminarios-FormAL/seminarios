abstract = """
Petri nets constitute a foundational model of concurrency, which makes their reversible counterparts particularly relevant. Reversible Petri nets have been used to investigate the relationships between different models of concurrency, such as process calculi and event structures. We address the problem of mechanizing the theory of reversible Petri nets with causally consistent reversibility. Specifically, I'll present a full mechanization of reversible Occurrence Petri nets in the Lean proof assistant. We formally verify that this model satisfies causal-dependent reversibility by mechanizing the so-called parabolic lemma.
